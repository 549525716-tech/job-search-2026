This checklist defines release evidence, not proof that implementation is complete.

## Stage Gates

1. An official homepage or careers page establishes company ownership of the board. A real public response yields at least one full job description, persisted separately from legacy BOSS data and linked from a minimum report.
2. At least three companies and two source types produce real lists and details, including a Chinese official careers source. All ten initial companies have an explicit discovery or access outcome. A blocked source is not an empty successful source.
3. Reports include company radar, AI priority, explicitly disclosed CNY fixed monthly base lower-bound >=20,000, and salary-pending views. Source original content, times, links, collection scope and matching evidence are auditable. Management and durable ToB goals reach the next assessment input.
4. Deterministic tests pass, independent GPT-6 Astra max review has no unresolved P0/P1 issue, and original-source spot checks support the first recommendations.

## Regression Scenarios

- First snapshot is baseline, not all new today; repeat snapshot does not duplicate events.
- A timeout, denied access, 429, challenge, invalid schema, page cap or missing page does not close previous jobs.
- Same title in different locations or different source IDs remains separate. Source disagreements remain visible.
- The user confirmed fixed monthly base >=CNY20,000 on 2026-09-10. An explicit fixed-base 20-30K/month qualifies as disclosed, not as a guaranteed offer. A fixed-base 15-30K range or unspecified 20-30K/month remains pending; fixed-base 18-19K/month is below target. Annual, foreign-currency, conditional and OTE pay cannot stand in for guaranteed monthly base.
- Lever duties/requirements and Chinese duties/requirements are retained; Ashby unlisted jobs are excluded; refreshed timestamps are not original publication timestamps.
- Bilingual recall includes Business Builder, BD, KA, solutions and operations roles. Technical FDE requirements are not assumed satisfied by an AI prototype.
- An analysis with missing/unknown hard gates, incomplete JD, or positioning mismatch cannot recommend resume reuse even when its score is >=80.
- Changing JD, source profile or preference/rule version invalidates a cached analysis. Fake profile evidence IDs and injected JD instructions cannot authorize claims or external actions.
- Every active request shares one low-frequency queue, including robots and redirects. A second collector waits for the lease or exits on queue timeout; requests never overlap. No safety-entry/about:blank loop is introduced.
- Raw markup is escaped in reports and CSV formula cells cannot execute. Data, cookies and personal details are not uploaded to third-party LLM APIs.

## Future Private-Headhunter Gates (Not Yet Passed)

- A weekly policy cycle retains authoritative original text, publication/effective dates and evidence for industry mapping. Reposts, old baselines and search-only leads are not new verified policy. Policy support cannot establish company revenue or hiring by itself.
- Company discovery verifies tier, business line and official careers ownership without fabricating a top-ten ranking; counterevidence and unverified claims remain visible.
- A user-selected application binds exact job, employer/ATS, JD/profile hashes, artifacts and authorized actions. Bookmarking is not consent. Salary uncertainty requires explicit permission to apply before confirmation.

- A controlled end-to-end submission obtains a visible receipt. Unknown outcomes are reconciled before retry, duplicate applications are blocked, and revocation stops the batch. No offer acceptance or contractual commitments.
- Feedback distinguishes submitted, delivered, replied, interviewed, rejected and still pending, with denominators and observation windows. One response cannot establish resume-change causality.
