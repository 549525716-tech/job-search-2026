OpenAI AI Sales Engineer
Company: OpenAI
Category: Frontier AI
OTE: $185K to $285K
Work model: Hybrid (San Francisco)
Estimated SE team size: 40 to 60 SEs

Company positioning
OpenAI builds and deploys frontier AI systems, including GPT-4, DALL-E, and the ChatGPT product family. The company operates both a consumer-facing product (ChatGPT with over 100 million weekly active users) and an enterprise API platform used by thousands of businesses to integrate language models, embeddings, and image generation into their own applications. The enterprise sales motion accelerated in 2024 with the launch of ChatGPT Enterprise and ChatGPT Team, creating significant demand for technical pre-sales talent who can bridge the gap between product capabilities and customer use cases.

AI products and platform
The core product line includes the GPT-4 family of large language models accessed through the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Enterprise for workplace deployment, ChatGPT Team for smaller organizations, fine-tuning capabilities for custom model training, the Assistants API for building AI agents, and Whisper for speech recognition. Enterprise customers range from financial services firms running compliance workflows to healthcare companies building clinical documentation tools.

Role details
AI Sales Engineers at OpenAI spend the majority of their time helping enterprise customers architect solutions on the OpenAI API platform. A typical week involves running technical deep-dives with engineering teams evaluating GPT-4 for their use cases, building proof-of-concept integrations that demonstrate API capabilities, and advising on prompt engineering, fine-tuning strategies, and token cost optimization. You would demo the API playground, walk through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures, explain embedding models for semantic search, and help customers understand the differences between model tiers. The role requires comfort with Python, REST APIs, and the ability to explain complex machine learning concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. You should understand transformer architecture at a conceptual level and be able to discuss topics like context windows, token limits, and model hallucination mitigation with credibility.

Technical skills and requirements
- Strong Python skills and experience building API integrations
- Understanding of LLM concepts: tokenization, embeddings, fine-tuning, RAG
- Ability to architect solutions combining OpenAI APIs with customer infrastructure
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Familiarity with vector databases and semantic search patterns

Interview process
OpenAI's interview process typically runs four to six weeks and includes a recruiter screen, a hiring manager conversation focused on sales methodology and deal experience, a technical deep-dive where you walk through a past solution architecture, a live demo exercise where you present an API-based solution to a panel, and a final round with cross-functional stakeholders. Candidates report that the demo exercise carries the most weight. They want to see that you can explain LLM capabilities clearly while handling technical objections about accuracy, latency, and cost. Preparation should include hands-on experience with the OpenAI API and at least one end-to-end project you can walk through in detail.

Salary and compensation
Base salary for AI Sales Engineers at OpenAI typically falls between $140,000 and $180,000, with on-target earnings (OTE) ranging from $185,000 to $285,000. The variable component is tied to enterprise deal outcomes and can exceed plan in strong quarters. Equity grants are significant given the company's private valuation. Benefits include premium health insurance, a generous 401(k) match, annual learning stipends, and meals at the San Francisco headquarters. Total compensation including equity refreshers can push well above $400,000 for experienced hires.
